The Artist

Self-portrait: I don't know what I am. I just go about it.

Kevin Beovides Casas

I have ventured into visual arts and literature from an interdisciplinary perspective. My artistic interests are linked to the new technologies and especially focused on the unconcealment of their communicational logic. My works have been included in art shows like "Ojalá!" (Zeitgenössische kubanische Kunst, Berlin, 2015) and Hope (ESMoA, Los Angeles, 2017). Founder of the project El Diletante Digital.

I have worked as a professor at the University of Arts of Cuba (ISA), the University of Havana and Miami Dade College. I have participated as a speaker in several events like Beyond the Hyphen. Approaching a Post Geographic Cuba (Princeton University, 2014) and the Permanent Seminar of the IGCAAV (Universidad de Guadalajara, 2017). My master's thesis was published in the book Outlooks on Digital Art (Mexico, 2020).

Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy, University of Havana (2004). Masters in Art History, University of Havana (2011). Associate in Science: AS Computer Programming and Analysis - Business Applications Programming, Miami Dade College (2019). I hold several certifications like: A+, Network+ and CCNA.

You can find my resume on linkedin and samples of my code on github.
